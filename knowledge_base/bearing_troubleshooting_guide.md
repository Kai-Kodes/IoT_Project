# Rolling Element Bearing Troubleshooting Guide (Deep Groove & Cylindrical Roller)
*DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL - Fictional Technical Reference for P_311*

## 1. Symptoms of Bearing Degradation
- Elevated RMS vibration exceeding 4.5 mm/s (ISO 10816 Zone C/D).
- Localized temperature rise at drive-end (DE) or non-drive-end (NDE) bearing cap exceeding 75°C to 95°C.
- High-frequency metallic chattering, growling, or clicking sound during rotation.
- Slight increase in motor current (10% to 25% above nominal) due to increased rolling friction.
- Fluctuations in motor rotational speed (RPM flutter ±15 to 30 RPM).

## 2. Plausible Root Causes
- Lubrication Failure: Inadequate lubrication, grease starvation, or excessive grease leading to churning and thermal runaway.
- Particulate Contamination: Moisture, metal shavings, or abrasive dust entering bearing races due to defective labyrinth seals.
- Fatigue Spalling: Micro-cracking and spalling on the inner race (BPFI), outer race (BPFO), or rolling elements (BSF).
- Misalignment / Preload: Angular or parallel shaft misalignment causing abnormal thrust loads and uneven race wear.
- Fluting from VFD Harmonics: Electrical discharge machining (EDM) fluting caused by circulating shaft currents.

## 3. Diagnostic Procedures
1. Perform shock-pulse or high-frequency demodulation analysis to detect early stage metal-to-metal contact.
2. Measure bearing housing temperature using a calibrated non-contact infrared pyrometer.
3. Collect grease sample from the drain port: check for discoloration, darkening, burnt smell, or suspended metal particles.
4. Rotate shaft manually by hand (with power locked out) to feel for mechanical notchiness, stiffness, or axial play.

## 4. Recommended Corrective Actions
- Immediate Action: If vibration exceeds 7.1 mm/s or temperature exceeds 90°C, shut down the machine to prevent catastrophic cage seizure.
- Intermediate Check: Purge old grease and replenish with 25g of high-temperature synthetic polyurea grease.
- Replacement: If raceway pitting or cage damage is confirmed, replace bearing set (SKF 6308-2Z or equivalent).
- Realignment: Perform laser alignment to ensure total angular deviation is under 0.05 mm / 100 mm.
