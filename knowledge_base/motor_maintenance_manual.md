# Industrial Electric Motor Maintenance Manual (Model EM-Series)
*DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL - Fictional Technical Reference for P_311*

## 1. Operating Specifications
- Machine Type: 3-Phase Squirrel Cage Induction Motor (400V, 50Hz, 4-Pole, 11 kW, TEFC)
- Nominal Full Load Current: 8.5 A to 9.2 A (Trip threshold: 14.0 A sustained)
- Nominal RPM: 1475 - 1490 RPM (Synchronous speed: 1500 RPM)
- Standard Operating Temperature: 45°C - 65°C (Insulation Class F, maximum limit 105°C)
- ISO 10816-3 Vibration Limits: Class I & II machines:
  - Zone A (Good): < 1.8 mm/s RMS
  - Zone B (Acceptable): 1.8 - 2.8 mm/s RMS
  - Zone C (Alert / Warning): 2.8 - 4.5 mm/s RMS
  - Zone D (Dangerous / Immediate Action): > 4.5 mm/s RMS
- Auxiliary Cooling Pressure: 3.8 - 4.5 bar nominal.

## 2. Safety Precautions
- ALWAYS perform Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on the main 400V circuit breaker prior to physical contact.
- Verify zero mechanical rotation and discharge any external power factor correction capacitors before maintenance.
- Allow housing to cool below 40°C before opening junction boxes or bearing housings.

## 3. General Maintenance Procedures
- Daily: Monitor vibration, surface temperature, and audible noise. Check cooling fan cowl for dust and debris.
- Monthly: Measure winding insulation resistance using a 500V Megohmmeter (minimum acceptable: > 10 Megohms).
- Quarterly: Grease bearings with lithium-complex EP2 grease (avoid over-greasing). Check hold-down bolt torque.
- Annually: Perform dynamic laser shaft alignment, rotor bar inspection, and stator surge testing.
