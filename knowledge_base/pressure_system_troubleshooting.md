# Motor Auxiliary Lubrication & Cooling Pressure System Guide
*DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL - Fictional Technical Reference for P_311*

## 1. Operating Pressure Specifications & Symptoms
- Nominal Lubrication / Cooling Circuit Pressure: 3.8 bar to 4.5 bar.
- Low Pressure Warning: Pressure falling below 3.0 bar.
- Low Pressure Critical Trip: Pressure falling below 2.0 bar (risk of hydrodynamic bearing collapse).
- Symptoms: Low pressure alarm, oil temperature increase, cavitating oil pump noise, oil foaming in sight glass.

## 2. Plausible Causes
- Blocked Lube Filter: Differential pressure across filter cartridge exceeding 1.2 bar due to particulate loading.
- Auxiliary Oil Pump Failure: Worn pump gear teeth, broken coupling drive, or electrical supply trip to pump motor.
- Circuit Leakage: Blown hydraulic hose, loose flare fitting, or damaged gasket along the cooling distribution manifold.
- Low Fluid Level: Sump oil reservoir volume below minimum operating gauge line.
- Pressure Relief Valve (PRV) Fault: Stuck-open relief valve causing fluid to bypass directly to sump without pressurization.

## 3. Diagnostic Procedures
1. Check differential pressure indicator on the duplex oil filter assembly.
2. Inspect oil reservoir sight glass for fluid level and presence of air entrainment/froth.
3. Walk the hydraulic line from pump to motor bearing housings to inspect for physical leaks or puddles.
4. Verify pressure sensor signal calibration (4-20 mA or 0-10V output against a calibrated mechanical test gauge).

## 4. Recommended Corrective Actions
- Immediate: Switch to standby duplex filter element if differential pressure is elevated.
- Maintenance: Top up lubricant reservoir with ISO VG 46/68 mineral turbine oil to the correct level.
- Repair: Tighten or replace leaking hydraulic couplings; replace worn pump seals.
- Sensor Check: If physical gauge reads 4.0 bar but telemetry shows 0.0 bar, replace faulty electronic pressure transmitter.
