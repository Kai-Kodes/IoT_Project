# Industrial Electric Motor Preventive Maintenance Checklist
*DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL - Fictional Technical Reference for P_311*

## 1. Shift Inspection Checklist (Every 8 Hours)
- [ ] Record baseline telemetry: Temperature (< 65°C), Vibration (< 2.8 mm/s RMS), Current (< 9.5 A).
- [ ] Verify cooling pressure gauge reads between 3.8 and 4.5 bar.
- [ ] Inspect motor fan cowl for loose debris, cardboard, plastic wraps, or dust blankets.
- [ ] Listen for abnormal acoustic emissions (high pitch whining, bearing squeak, rhythmic pulsing).
- [ ] Touch housing cautiously to detect unexpected localized hot spots or excessive vibration.

## 2. Weekly Checklist
- [ ] Measure vibration at drive end and non-drive end in horizontal, vertical, and axial planes.
- [ ] Check oil level sight glass on forced lubrication unit; inspect oil clarity.
- [ ] Inspect motor terminal box cable glands for strain or loose earth bonding cables.
- [ ] Clean external stator heat-sink fins with compressed air.

## 3. Monthly / Quarterly Checklist
- [ ] Measure stator winding insulation resistance (Megger test: 500V DC for 1 minute; threshold > 10 Megohms).
- [ ] Inspect motor hold-down anchor bolts and retorque to 85 Nm.
- [ ] Inspect flexible coupling elastomeric element for signs of wear, tearing, or ozone cracking.
- [ ] Replenish bearing grease according to manufacturer schedule (25g per bearing; do not over-fill).

## 4. Annual Overhaul
- [ ] Complete laser shaft alignment check between motor and driven equipment (tolerance < 0.05 mm).
- [ ] Replace bearing sets if machine has exceeded 20,000 continuous operating hours.
- [ ] Dynamic balancing check of rotor and cooling fan assembly.
- [ ] Clean and revarnish stator winding if insulation resistance indicates thermal degradation.
