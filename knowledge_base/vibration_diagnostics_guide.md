# Industrial Motor Vibration Diagnostics Guide
*DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL - Fictional Technical Reference for P_311*

## 1. Vibration Severity Standards (ISO 10816-3)
- Velocity RMS (10 Hz to 1000 Hz):
  - < 1.8 mm/s: Excellent (Zone A) - newly commissioned machine.
  - 1.8 to 2.8 mm/s: Normal (Zone B) - acceptable for unrestricted long-term operation.
  - 2.8 to 4.5 mm/s: Advisory / Warning (Zone C) - satisfactory for short-term operation; plan scheduled inspection.
  - 4.5 to 7.1 mm/s: Alarm (Zone D lower) - significant fault developing; investigate immediately.
  - > 7.1 mm/s: Trip Danger (Zone D upper) - immediate shutdown required to prevent structural damage.

## 2. Vibration Fault Classification
- 1X RPM Dominant Peak: Rotor unbalance, bent shaft, or eccentric coupling.
- 2X RPM Dominant Peak: Mechanical misalignment (angular/parallel) or loose motor base bolts ("soft foot").
- Sub-synchronous Peaks (0.4X to 0.48X RPM): Oil whirl, cage instability, or structural resonance.
- Non-synchronous High Frequencies (> 5X RPM): Rolling element impact frequencies (BPFO, BPFI).

## 3. Diagnostic Procedures
1. Check hold-down foundation bolts with a calibrated torque wrench (ensure specified torque: 85 Nm).
2. Measure "soft foot" using dial indicators before tightening motor mounting feet (tolerance < 0.05 mm).
3. Record 3-axis vibration (Horizontal, Vertical, Axial) to isolate directional stiffness and coupling misalignment.
4. Verify balance quality grade (G2.5 per ISO 1940 standard).

## 4. Recommended Actions
- For Unbalance: Clean debris from cooling fan blades and conduct in-situ dynamic balancing.
- For Misalignment: Re-shim the foundation pads and perform laser shaft alignment.
- For Mechanical Looseness: Retorque foundation anchor bolts and inspect bedplate for concrete cracks.
